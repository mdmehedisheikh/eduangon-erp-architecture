## 🎓 Platform Overview
EduAngon is an enterprise institutional ERP built on **Next.js 15** and **Cloudflare D1**. It addresses the administrative complexities of Bangladesh's education system, where institutions often combine General National Curriculum, Dakhil/Alim Madrasa streams, and auxiliary coaching shifts under one organization.

### Core Capabilities
1. **Tri-Stream Academic Hierarchy:** Configurable grade and syllabus structures supporting English/Bangla Medium, Madrasa (Hifz/Kitab divisions), and batch-wise coaching centers.
2. **Double-Entry Tuition Ledger:** Handles monthly fees, exam fees, transport charges, scholarships, and installment waivers with detailed audit logs.
3. **Bangla Typography & UX:** Designed specifically with native Bengali font rendering (Noto Serif Bengali / SolaimanLipi) for report cards, fee slips, and ID card generation.
